# Cheerio GTM Use-Case Mapper

A GTM intelligence prototype that identifies potential customer-workflow opportunities for Cheerio AI using publicly observable company signals.

## Problem

Cheerio AI solves different customer-engagement and workflow problems across industries such as D2C, BFSI, Real Estate, and Healthcare.

The GTM challenge is not simply finding companies that use WhatsApp or automation.

The challenge is understanding:

- What the company's customer journey looks like
- What is already automated
- Where communication or workflow complexity exists
- Where a potential Cheerio use case may exist

## Approach

Instead of starting with a generic lead score, this prototype follows an evidence-first approach:

Company Discovery
→ Customer Journey Research
→ Existing Capability Detection
→ Workflow Gap Identification
→ Cheerio Use-Case Hypothesis

## Research Sample

Built an initial prospect universe across four verticals:

- D2C / E-commerce
- BFSI
- Real Estate
- Healthcare

A smaller sample is deeply researched to validate the methodology before scaling.

## Tech Stack

- Clay — company discovery and enrichment
- AI research — customer journey and workflow analysis
- Public web data — evidence collection

## Current Research Signals

### WhatsApp Channel Usage
Identifies how a company uses WhatsApp, if publicly observable.

### Customer Journey
Maps the observable journey from acquisition/inquiry through conversion and post-purchase engagement.

## Key Principle

Existing automation does not automatically mean low opportunity.

The goal is to identify incremental workflow opportunities, not companies with zero automation.
