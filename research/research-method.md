# Research Method

## Objective

Identify potential Cheerio AI workflow opportunities using an evidence-first GTM research process.

The objective is to understand customer journeys and workflow complexity before defining an ICP or opportunity score.

## 1. Company Discovery

Created an initial prospect universe of **50 companies** across four verticals:

- D2C / E-commerce — 15
- BFSI — 15
- Real Estate — 10
- Healthcare — 10

## 2. Data Validation

The Clay results contained geographic mismatches.

Companies outside the intended India-focused research scope were removed.

**50 discovered → 41 retained**

## 3. Research Sampling

The MVP did not deeply research all 41 companies.

Instead, **3 companies were randomly selected from each vertical**.

**4 verticals × 3 companies = 12 researched accounts**

This was sufficient to test the methodology without overbuilding the prototype.

## 4. Vertical-Specific Research

Research columns were customized according to the relevant Cheerio workflow.

### D2C

Product discovery → conversational commerce → purchase → repeat engagement

### BFSI

Customer lifecycle → communication → follow-up → retention

### Real Estate

Lead/inquiry → communication → follow-up → booking → post-sale

### Healthcare

Patient/customer inquiry → service/order → follow-up → retention

## 5. Workflow Assessment

Each researched company was assessed on:

- Journey Complexity
- Communication Intensity
- Channel Fragmentation
- Critical Business Event
- Automation Maturity
- Workflow Friction
- Research Confidence

## 6. Scoring

Five signals contribute to the 100-point Opportunity Score:

- Journey Complexity — 25
- Communication Intensity — 20
- Channel Fragmentation — 15
- Critical Business Event — 25
- Workflow Friction — 15

Automation Maturity is contextual.

Research Confidence is tracked separately.

## 7. Evidence Framework

**Observed**

Supported by public evidence.

**Not Found**

Could not be verified publicly.

**Potential Gap**

A hypothesis based on observable workflow characteristics.

A missing public feature is not treated as proof that the capability does not exist internally.

## Core Principle

The system does not equate technology adoption with GTM fit.

Instead:

**Customer Journey + Communication Complexity + Business Event + Workflow Friction**

are used to identify potential opportunities.
